About Cyber Fraud Awareness Portal

The Cyber Fraud Awareness Portal is a web-based platform developed to spread awareness about cyber crimes and online frauds. It helps users understand common cyber threats such as phishing attacks, UPI fraud, fake calls, online shopping scams, and social media fraud.

Users can learn safety tips, search for useful information, and use an online demonstration form to report cyber fraud incidents.

The portal promotes cyber safety and encourages responsible use of the internet.

This project is developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Cyber Fraud?

Cyber fraud is an online crime where attackers steal money, personal information or passwords.

2. What is Phishing?

Phishing uses fake emails, SMS messages or websites to steal sensitive information.

3. How can I report cyber fraud?

Use the Report Fraud page for this project demo. For real financial cyber fraud in India, call 1930.

4. Should I share my OTP?

No. Never share your OTP, UPI PIN or banking password with anyone.

5. What is the Cyber Crime Helpline?

In India, 1930 is the helpline for financial cyber fraud.
